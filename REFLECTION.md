# Reflection: AI-Assisted Development Experience

## How Reviewing AI-Generated Cards Differed from Writing Them Yourself

Reviewing AI-generated content required a fundamentally different mindset than creating content from scratch. When writing manually, I was building understanding incrementally, researching each concept before explaining it. With AI assistance, the challenge shifted to critical evaluation—verifying technical accuracy, checking for hallucinated information, and ensuring the content met educational objectives rather than just sounding authoritative.

The review process demanded deeper technical knowledge because I had to catch subtle errors that might slip past someone less familiar with CSS. While manual writing involved creative problem-solving and structure decisions, AI review required systematic verification against multiple sources and rigorous fact-checking.

## What Surprised Me About AI Output

**Positive surprises:** The AI generated remarkably coherent explanations and understood complex CSS relationships like the interaction between `shape-outside` and `clip-path`. The code examples were syntactically correct and followed modern CSS practices. The AI also maintained consistent tone and structure matching the repository style.

**Negative surprises:** Browser compatibility information required careful verification—AI can present outdated or overly optimistic support data. The AI also tended toward overly technical language that needed simplification for educational purposes. Real-world application examples were sometimes generic rather than truly practical.

## Aspects Requiring Human Expertise

Critical thinking proved essential for evaluating technical accuracy and educational value. Human judgment was necessary for determining appropriate difficulty levels, ensuring accessibility considerations were comprehensive, and verifying that examples would actually work in practice. The AI couldn't assess whether explanations would effectively teach the concept to students at the target skill level.

Quality assurance required human expertise in testing code examples, cross-referencing multiple authoritative sources, and maintaining repository standards that extend beyond simple formatting rules.

## Future Applications in Development

This experience mirrors emerging industry patterns where developers increasingly manage AI-generated code rather than writing everything manually. The skills developed—systematic review, technical verification, and quality assurance—are becoming as valuable as coding ability itself. In professional settings, this could accelerate development while maintaining quality through human oversight, particularly for documentation, testing, and maintenance tasks where AI can handle routine work while humans focus on architecture and critical evaluation.
